Privacy policy

BenBenClipper · last updated 22 October 2026

BenBenClipper is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only with YouTube channels that belong to its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When a YouTube channel is connected through Google's sign-in screen, BenBenClipper can:

  • upload videos to that channel and set their title, description and publishing time;
  • read that channel's name, subscriber count and view counts, and the statistics of its own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to show the owner their channels' statistics.
  • Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Statistics are kept for at most 30 days.

BenBenClipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting BenBenClipper's folder removes all data it stored.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
